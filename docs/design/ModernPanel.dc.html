Every row states the format, not just the language — the whole reason to open this panel is to answer “which of these is the lossless one?”
Overlay, not a dialogPanel floats over the frame, playback never pauses, and the video stays visible behind it — you can hear the track you just picked while the list is still open.
Track metadataCodec, channels, bitrate and sample rate come straight from the pad caps and stream tags. Language names use the file's own tag, rendered in its native script.
External subtitlesSidecar files are found by stem match in the same directory only — no directory walking, no network fetch. Both sources appear in one list.
Video pipeline rowThe interesting half of the panel for anyone who cares: what the shader is doing and whether hardware decode actually engaged. Green dot = accelerated, amber = software fallback.
DismissalEsc, the close glyph, or a click on the video. Never auto-dismisses on a track change — people switch two things in a row.
