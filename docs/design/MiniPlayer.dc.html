Mini player
C1 · 420 × 236 · always on top
Sintel — 2010
Three transport controls and a bar. Everything else lives in the full window — a 420 px frame that tries to keep the settings panel is a worse full window, not a mini one.
Pin & restorePin toggles always-on-top; the diagonal arrows return to the full window at the exact same playback position. Close stops playback outright.
Window behaviourAspect locked to the stream; resize from any edge scales the whole frame. Drag anywhere on the picture to move — the frame has no title bar to grab.
ChromeBoth scrims are hover-only and fade at 1.2 s — faster than the full window, because the frame is small enough that chrome eats a real share of it.
Same surfaceThis is the same wgpu widget at a different size, not a second render path — one shader, one decode pipeline, one set of colour rules.
